# Changes

Newest first. Each release lists only what changed since the previous one.

## v1.0.0 — unreleased

### Added
- **Quick transfer hints**: the key hints line of the open inventory now lists
  `Shift + LMB`, `Shift + Ctrl + LMB` and `Shift + RMB`.
- **Quick craft one**: `Shift + Click` in recipes list.
- **Craft amount**: `Alt + Click` on the Craft button — choose how many times to craft.
- **Craft all**: `Ctrl + Click` on the Craft button — craft until the ingredients run out.
- `Esc` stops both.
- Attempt to craft on unpowered station switches the station on automatically,
  and switches it off again when its crafting is over.

### Changed
- **No items on the floor**: crafting results and harvested animals/mushrooms now go to your
  inventory in the base game, so the mod only handles item actions in containers
  (e.g. breaking glass in a bottle crate).

## v0.9.0 — 2026-10-10

First public release.

### Added
- **Quick transfer**: `Shift + Click` moves a stack, `Shift + Right Click` moves one item
- **Move all similar**: `Shift + Ctrl + Click` moves every stack of that item.
- **Break all / Slaughter all**: `Shift + Break` / `Shift + Slaughter` acts on the whole
  stack or on all animals of the same kind.
- **"Stack" button** - added in the storage panel (`Shift + Click`: also from the backpack,
  `Alt + Click`: every item the container accepts) and "Take similar" (`Alt + Take all`).
- **Plant max**: `Shift + Click` on a recipe plants as many as possible.
- **No items on the floor** - Results of crafting, item actions and harvesting go to your inventory instead
  of the floor when a container is full.
